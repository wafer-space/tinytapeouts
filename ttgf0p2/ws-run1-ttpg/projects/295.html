How this design grew

KianV RISC-V Linux SoC has been made on 10 shuttles since Nov 2023. Every version, oldest first, with this one in bold:

  1. Nov 2023 · Tiny Tapeout 5 KianV uLinux SoC — 8×2 tiles
  2. Apr 2024 · Tiny Tapeout 6 KianV uLinux SoC — 8×2 tiles
  3. Jun 2024 · Tiny Tapeout 7 KianV RISC-V RV32E Baremetal SoC — 3×2 tiles, RV32E bare-metal version
  4. Nov 2024 · Tiny Tapeout IHP 0.2 KianV RISC-V RV32E Baremetal SoC — 4×2 tiles, RV32E bare-metal version
  5. Nov 2025 · Tiny Tapeout GF 0.2 KianV uLinux SoC — 4×4 tiles
  6. Nov 2025 · Tiny Tapeout GF 0.1 KianV uLinux SoC — in a shared block
  7. Dec 2025 · wafer.space Run 1 KIAN KianV: A 32-bit RISC-V Linux SoC taped out on GF180MCU — 1×1 slot, 5 V standard cells and SRAM
  8. Mar 2026 · Tiny Tapeout IHP 26a KianV SV32 TT Linux SoC — 8×4 tiles
  9. Jul 2026 · Tiny Tapeout GF 0.3 KianV uLinux SoC — 4×4 tiles
  10. Jul 2026 · wafer.space Run 2 KIAN KianV: A 32-bit RISC-V Linux SoC taped out on GF180MCU Run 2 — 1×1 slot, 3.3 V standard cells and SRAM
