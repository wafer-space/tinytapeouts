From a Tiny Tapeout tile to a whole die

FABulous FPGA went on to a slot of its own on a wafer.space run. When a design outgrows its tiles, a whole die on a wafer.space run is the next step.

Want your own design on a chip like this one? Tiny Tapeout is a cheap and easy way to get started: see tinytapeout.com.

Tiny Tapeout and wafer.space are independent companies that work together (announcement). Tiny Tapeout runs its own shuttles: it takes the projects, builds the die, and sells and supports the chips and boards. wafer.space has the die manufactured on its GF180MCU runs, and these pages are wafer.space's measurements of it. For anything about a Tiny Tapeout project, chip or order, contact Tiny Tapeout.
