How this design grew

FABulous FPGA has been made on 9 shuttles since Dec 2022. Every version, oldest first, with this one in bold:

  1. Dec 2022 · Google GFMPW-0 56C6 One-hot FABulous FPGA — Caravel chip, a one-hot FPGA built with the FABulous framework
  2. Dec 2025 · wafer.space Run 1 MOLE FABulous FPGA — 1×1 slot
  3. Mar 2026 · Tiny Tapeout IHP 26a Tiny FABulous FPGA — 8×4 tiles
  4. May 2026 · Tiny Tapeout SKY 26a Tiny FABulous FPGA — 8×4 tiles
  5. May 2026 · Tiny Tapeout SKY 26b Tiny FABulous FPGA — 8×4 tiles
  6. Jul 2026 · Tiny Tapeout GF 0.3 Tiny FABulous FPGA (3.3V version) — 4×4 tiles, 3.3 V version Tiny FABulous FPGA — 4×4 tiles
  7. Jul 2026 · wafer.space Run 2 MOLE FABulous FPGA — 1×1 slot
  8. Sep 2026 · Tiny Tapeout SKY 26c Tiny FABulous FPGA — 8×4 tiles
  9. Sep 2026 · Tiny Tapeout IHP 26b Tiny FABulous FPGA — 6×4 tiles

From a Tiny Tapeout tile to a whole die

FABulous FPGA started as a few tiles on Tiny Tapeout and went on to dies of its own on wafer.space Run 1 and wafer.space Run 2. When your design outgrows its tiles, a whole die on a wafer.space run is the next step.

Want your own design on a chip like this one?

Start with a tile: the next Tiny Tapeout GF180MCU chips take projects until 7 December 2026, for wafer.space Run 3. For a die of your own, wafer.space Run 3 takes orders until 9 December 2026.

Submit a project

Tiny Tapeout and wafer.space are independent companies that work together (announcement). Tiny Tapeout runs its own shuttles: it takes the projects, builds the die, and sells and supports the chips and boards. wafer.space has the dies manufactured on its GF180MCU runs, and these pages are wafer.space's measurements of them. For anything about a Tiny Tapeout project, chip or order, contact Tiny Tapeout.
