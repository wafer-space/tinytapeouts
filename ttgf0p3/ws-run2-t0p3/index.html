Data and method

Everything on this page is measured from the public reticle layout

  • Logic cells are standard cell instances, excluding fillers, taps, antenna diodes and tie cells.
  • Transistors are places where gate polysilicon crosses diffusion, not counting dummy fill.
  • Core area: Core area is the die minus a 350 µm pad ring on each side.
  • Theoretical maximum tiles one cell at its placement boundary with no routing.
  • Densest mm² is the best 0.1 mm square anywhere in each die, sliding in 20 µm steps and avoiding SRAM, given per mm².
