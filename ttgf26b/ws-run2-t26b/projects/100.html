GF180mcuD digital Physical Unclonable Function: 8 distinct-tap maximal-length 10-bit LFSRs act as synthesizable virtual ring-oscillators, a 6-bit challenge selects a pair, and an arbiter FSM compares their popcounts over a programmable 2^N-cycle window (N in 4..12) to produce a response bit. 64 challenges accumulate into a 64-bit response that can be XORed with a host-loaded memristor vector for dual-entropy mode. SPI-programmable, IRQ output, 25 MHz signoff clock, 1x2 tile (upsized from 1x1: global-placement utilization 103.6% on 1x1).
