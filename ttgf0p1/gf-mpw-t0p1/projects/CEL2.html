Standard cell test in the foundry's 7-track 5 V cells with the thick-oxide layers removed, so they are made as 3.3 V transistors. A chain of 1,024 inverters delays a Manchester-coded counter, so the speed of the cells can be measured on the chip from its output pin; each pair of cell tests has a core supply of its own.
