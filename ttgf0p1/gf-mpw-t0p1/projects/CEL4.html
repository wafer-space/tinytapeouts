Standard cell test in Oklahoma State University's 9-track 3.3 V library (gf180mcu_osu_sc_gp9t3v3), with its wells and implants redrawn. A chain of 1,024 inverters delays a Manchester-coded counter, so the speed of the cells can be measured on the chip from its output pin; each pair of cell tests has a core supply of its own.
